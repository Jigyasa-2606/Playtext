Playtext Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Playtext is a Chrome extension that turns a YouTube video into a readable transcript. It is built to do that on your computer. It does not create an account and it does not run ads.

What Playtext reads

When you click Generate, Playtext reads the YouTube video page and the captions or transcript already attached to that video. It only does this after you ask it to, for the link or tab you chose.

What Playtext stores

The last transcript you generated is saved in Chrome’s local extension storage on your device so you can copy it, search it, or open the full view. That copy stays on your computer. Playtext does not upload transcripts to our servers. Playtext does not have its own backend.

What Playtext does not collect

  • No name, email, or login
  • No analytics or advertising trackers
  • No sale or sharing of personal data

Permissions

Playtext asks for access to YouTube pages so it can fetch captions. It uses storage to keep the last transcript on your device, and it uses tab and scripting access so Generate can read the video you already have open.

Contact

If you have a privacy question about Playtext, open an issue on the project page where you installed or downloaded the extension, or contact the developer who published this listing.
